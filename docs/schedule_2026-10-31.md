## What moves the dates

1. New iterations from the phone — the biggest factor, see pace above.
2. Paid Apple Developer account: a hard gate for 34. Alexey 31.10:
   «Оформлю в ноябре».
3. Cloud keys (Google, Dropbox) — Alexey creates the apps himself; plan § 31
   says a provider without a key is shown honestly as disconnected, so 31 can
   close with Yandex only.
4. Session limits of the Claude plan (rule: heavy steps one at a time).
